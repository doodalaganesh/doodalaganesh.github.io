Workforce360

AI-powered HR & Workforce Intelligence

Business Perspective

  • Proactive workforce planning with predictive hiring and attrition intelligence
  • Natural-language access to workforce data beyond static reports

The Business Problem

HR and workforce teams often have valuable information spread across multiple reports, spreadsheets, dashboards, and operational sources — making it difficult to get a complete view of hiring activity, understand changing demand, identify attrition risks early, or answer workforce questions without manual reporting.

The challenge is not simply having data — it is turning that data into timely and understandable information for decision-making.

The Solution

This platform brings workforce analytics, predictive intelligence, and AI-assisted information access into a single system — with executive dashboards, forecasting, attrition risk analysis, recruiter insights, and natural-language querying of workforce data.

Problem Areas

  • Get a complete view of hiring and workforce activity
  • Understand changing hiring demand
  • Identify potential attrition risks early
  • Track recruitment and fulfillment performance
  • Compare workforce trends across teams, accounts, or business areas
  • Quickly answer HR questions without manually preparing reports
  • Turn large amounts of workforce data into actionable insights

Platform Capabilities

  • Executive workforce dashboards
  • Hiring and recruitment analytics
  • Demand and fulfillment forecasting
  • Early attrition risk analysis
  • Recruiter performance insights
  • Cost and workforce metrics
  • AI-generated business insights
  • Natural-language querying of workforce data

Key Business Capabilities

Workforce & Hiring Intelligence — Visibility into hiring demand, fulfillment, recruitment pipelines, workforce activity, and important KPIs.

Predictive Workforce Planning — Forecasts future hiring demand and fulfillment trends using historical workforce patterns.

Early Attrition Intelligence — Machine learning identifies employees with higher likelihood of early attrition for earlier HR investigation.

Recruiter Performance Analytics — Recruiter-level performance analysis and predictive indicators to improve recruitment effectiveness.

AI-Powered Business Insights — Concise insights from dashboard data so users understand trends without manually analyzing every chart.

Natural-Language HR Assistant — Ask workforce-related questions in natural language and receive answers from organizational data.

Example Business Questions

  • How is hiring demand changing over time?

  • What is the expected fulfillment trend for upcoming periods?

Combines business intelligence, predictive analytics, machine learning, and Generative AI into one workforce intelligence platform — moving beyond static reporting toward understanding workforce data, identifying emerging patterns, forecasting demand, and interacting with business information using natural language.

Modular AI workflow that separates access control, intent understanding, retrieval, document processing, and response generation.

03 INSURANCE / CLAIMS AI

InsureAI

AI-Powered Insurance Claims Intelligence

Business Perspective

  • Automated claim risk screening with scores, categories, and plain-English explanations
  • Risk-based claim routing so reviewers focus on higher-risk submissions
  • AI assistant and analytics dashboard for policy and claims support

The Business Problem

Insurance teams process large amounts of policy and claims information, and claim evaluation can involve repetitive checks before a claim reaches the appropriate review stage.

A typical workflow may require teams to review customer and policy information, understand incident descriptions, compare claim amounts against coverage, identify unusual patterns, decide which claims need additional review, respond to common questions, and monitor claim volumes and risk trends.

When handled manually, straightforward claims can take unnecessary effort while potentially higher-risk claims may require additional attention. The challenge is to make the initial claim-review process faster, more consistent, and easier to manage without removing human oversight.

The Solution

InsureAI provides a digital workflow where customers manage policies and submit claims, while the system automatically performs an initial AI-assisted risk assessment.

When a claim is submitted, the platform evaluates available information and produces a risk score, risk category, plain-English explanation, and recommended workflow state for further review — plus an AI assistant for common questions and an analytics dashboard for monitoring claim activity.

Key Business Capabilities

Automated Claim Risk Screening — Every submitted claim is evaluated for potential risk indicators, considering claim-to-coverage ratio and incident description, presented as an easy-to-understand score and explanation.

Risk-Based Claim Review — Higher-risk claims automatically move into a review state so human reviewers focus on claims requiring investigation. The system does not make final approval or rejection decisions.

Policy & Claims Management — Structured relationships between Customer → Policy → Claim with policy type, coverage amount, premium, dates, and status.

AI Insurance Assistant — Conversational support for filing claims, premiums, coverage, claim status, and policy cancellation — using rule-based or optional LLM-powered responses.

Claims Analytics — Dashboard indicators including total claims, approved payouts, high-risk claims, and average risk score for operational visibility.

Example Business Workflow

Customer submits claim → Policy validated → Claim analyzed → Risk score generated → Risk category assigned → Claim enters appropriate workflow → Human review where required

If a claim is identified as high risk, the system automatically assigns it to a review state rather than treating it as a normal pending claim.

Business Value

  • Faster Initial Screening — Automates first-level risk assessment and reduces repetitive manual checks
  • Better Risk Visibility — Clear risk score and explanation for each evaluated claim
  • Focused Human Review — Helps reviewers prioritize claims requiring closer attention
  • Improved Customer Support — Quick answers to common policy and claims questions
  • Better Operational Visibility — Analytics for monitoring volumes, payouts, and risk patterns
  • Flexible AI Adoption — Operates without paid LLM dependency using rule-based logic, with optional LLM for richer reasoning

Demonstrates how AI can be embedded directly into a real business workflow — Insurance Data → Claim Submission → AI Risk Analysis → Risk-Based Workflow → Human Review → Analytics — creating an end-to-end example of AI + software + business workflow integration.

Technical Overview

Frontend → FastAPI → Business APIs → SQL Database · Claim Submission → AI Risk Agent → Risk Score → Claim Storage

High-Level Architecture

Three-tier architecture: frontend application, FastAPI backend, and relational database — with AI integrated into claim-processing and conversational workflows.

User Question → AI Assistant → Response

Frontend Layer

Single-page web interface with Dashboard, Claims, File a Claim, Policies, and AI Assistant — displaying summary information, risk indicators, claim statuses, and AI-generated results after claim processing.

API & Backend Layer

  • Python · FastAPI · Uvicorn · Pydantic · SQLAlchemy

REST endpoints for customers, policies, claims, analytics, AI chat, and health checks — handling validation, database operations, AI processing, and API responses.

Relational Data Model

Customer → Policy → Claim

Claim records store original information and AI-generated intelligence: claim amount, incident description, incident date, status, risk score, risk label, and AI explanation.

AI Risk Scoring Layer

Core intelligence performing Claim Risk Scoring and Insurance FAQ Assistance — receiving claim amount, policy coverage, and incident description to produce Risk Score → Risk Label → Explanation (0–100, categorized as Low, Medium, or High).

Dual AI Architecture

Rule-Based Mode — Transparent logic considering claim-to-coverage ratio, suspicious phrases, and short descriptions — no external AI API required.

LLM Mode — Optional OpenAI integration for richer claim reasoning, returning Score + Risk Label + Reason — with fallback to rule-based approach on failure.

Claim Processing Pipeline

Claim submitted → Backend validation → AI analysis → Risk generation → Workflow assignment → Database persistence → Frontend response

High-risk claims automatically move to review state; entire workflow executes synchronously during claim submission.

Conversational AI Pipeline

User Question → Chat API → AI Assistant → FAQ / LLM Processing → Response

Supports claims, premiums, coverage, claim status, and policy cancellation via rule-based or LLM backends.

Analytics Layer

Aggregates claim information for dashboard metrics: claim volume, approved payouts, high-risk count, and average risk score.

Database & Deployment

  • Development: SQLite · Production: PostgreSQL
  • SQLAlchemy ORM · Docker · Docker Compose · Python 3.11

Single-container deployment design with PostgreSQL as the production database option.

Embeds AI directly into insurance workflows rather than as a standalone chatbot — designed as a demonstration application showcasing AI + software + business workflow integration with transparent rule-based logic and optional LLM enrichment.
